# Cold OSRS player review - companion build 001

- Reviewer: Cold OSRS Player hat (24, main + HCIM, no AI tool subscriptions, scrolls fast).
- Date: 2026-06-21.
- Linear: [RAI-72](https://linear.app/rainnworks/issue/RAI-72).
- Scope: the just-shipped companion surface (marketing site sections, og card, in-plugin first-touch config, dialogue cooldowns, backend archetypes), as seen by someone who landed cold from a `/r/2007scape` thread or a YouTube comment.

## Verdict

Would install free. Would not pay £7 yet. Would close the tab on the og-card before even hitting the site if the rotation lands me on `apps/marketing/public/og-card-companion.svg:50` ("Protect magic. She nukes 50s the second the orb spawns.") because that one specific line reads like the engineer wrote it. Inside the plugin I would probably uninstall in five minutes because the four-starter chooser at `apps/plugin/docs/CONFIG.md:106-116` is the product picking my friend for me on rails.

## What hooks me

1. **The H1 trade.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:138` - "Stop alt-tabbing. Start playing." That is one trade, plain English, and every OSRS player who has alt-tabbed to the wiki mid-Vorkath knows exactly what it means. No "AI", no "powered by", no jargon. This single line is the strongest sales surface on the page.
2. **The bot/ban answer.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/FAQ.tsx:16` - "Tibbly never synthesises mouse or keyboard input, never moves your character, never clicks for you... the same shape as Quest Helper or WikiSync, which 555K and 302K players run respectively." This is what every OSRS player wants to know first, and the answer cites real plugin counts. Specific numbers beat hedged claims.
3. **The Hub-rejection callout.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/FAQ.tsx:56` - "exactly the shape the Hub maintainers asked for in the rejection notes on the previous OSRS MCP plugin PR." That tells me you have skin in the game, you have already been rejected once, and you know what changed. That is community-fluent in a way a brochure cannot fake.
4. **The Iron tier hidden behind email.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/PricingTiers.tsx:226-234` - burying the £49 tier as "email hello@tibbly.app if you want it" is a power move. It signals confidence and makes the £19 Pro feel like the cap, not the squeeze. I respect that.
5. **The dragon-shield example.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:250-251` - "Anti-dragon shield is in your bank tab 7, I tagged it." That is the *specific* claim that makes me believe the product knows my game state. Not "I can read your inventory." It told me the tab and said it tagged it. That is the magic moment for me on the page.

## What bounces me

1. **The og-card line.** `apps/marketing/public/og-card-companion.svg:50` - "Protect magic. She nukes 50s the second the orb spawns." This is the line a Reddit reader sees before they click the link. Two problems. First, Vorkath is a male dragon, not a "she", and the players who care about Vorkath WILL catch this in two seconds. Second, the phrasing "nukes 50s" is engineer-flavoured. A real player says "she hits 50s" or "she's plus-50s". "Nukes" reads as machine-translation of "deals high damage". The line that should be the social-share grab is the one that will get screenshotted on Reddit with "lol this AI thinks Vorkath is a girl."
2. **The og-card pronoun.** `apps/marketing/public/og-card-companion.svg:1` - same pronoun problem in the aria-label: "She watches what you watch and speaks when there is something to say." The companion is "she" on the og-card but "it" on `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:110` ("It watches what you watch"). Pick one. Inconsistency reads as careless, and a cold visitor noticing this in the first 30 seconds wonders what else is sloppy.
3. **The "ten minutes ago" line.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:97` and `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:60` - "You hit 99 Slayer ten minutes ago. I noticed." On the final hit of a hellhound at 99 Slayer the player just watched their own level-up fireworks. They know they hit 99. The companion telling them "ten minutes ago" past tense is a weird beat - either congratulate me now or stay quiet. "I noticed" reads as the companion fishing for warmth points and not landing.
4. **The £7 price tag below the H1.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/PricingTiers.tsx:43-45` - "Full companion: voice, memory, reactive lines" is the only thing the Hobbyist tier offers over Free. The Free tier already has the "core tool surface (find_item, ge_price, wiki)". So I am paying £7/month for the companion alone. Fine, that is the bet. But the page does not yet make the companion the *visible* product. It is one section out of nine, and the hero is still "Stop alt-tabbing. Start playing" which is the chatbot pitch. The marketing has not picked the relationship product hard enough yet.
5. **The "watermarked replies" smell.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/PricingTiers.tsx:38` - "Watermarked replies" on Free tier reads like a Saas decision rather than a player-felt boundary. I do not know what a watermarked reply looks like and the page does not show me. If the watermark is "powered by Tibbly", just say so. If it is something cuter, show me.
6. **The placeholder Probe sprite in marketing context.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/CompanionSprite.tsx:25-122` - the rendered SVG is fine engineering but reads as a brown sphere with a yellow eye at sticker size. On a page selling "your OSRS friend", the friend looks like a Bandos chestplate that someone drew a circle on. There is no warmth in the silhouette. The companion is going to be next to me for months and the first thing I see is a brown ball.
7. **The "every starter is called Probe" reality.** `apps/plugin/docs/CONFIG.md:108-112` and `docs/product/COMPANION_VISUAL_BIBLE.md:46-71` - "Probe (default)", "Probe - comm visor", "Probe - heavy armor", "Probe - research array". The four starters are tints of one mesh. The marketing page calls them "starters" (plural friends) but the in-plugin reality is "one robot with four hats". That gap will close hard the moment a player opens the config panel. They were promised "the only OSRS plugin you'd say goodbye to", they got a colour swatch.
8. **The default ON for proactive lines.** `apps/plugin/src/main/kotlin/co/rowm/osrsllm/companion/CompanionConfig.kt:30` - `proactiveTriggersEnabled: Boolean = true`. The first thing the cooldown gates explain at `apps/plugin/src/main/kotlin/co/rowm/osrsllm/companion/CompanionDialogueOrchestrator.kt:14-32` is how *not* to be Clippy. The first thing the config says is "on by default". The second proactive line of my session, no matter how good, is going to feel like the AI is showing off. Either ship it default-off or make the first proactive line *earn* the next one.
9. **The Hobbyist features list.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/PricingTiers.tsx:48-52` - "Casual daily play, no maths" / "Hard cap at the right ceiling for the tier". "No maths" is engineer-speak for "we hide the token math from you", and "the right ceiling for the tier" is literally a tautology. These are placeholder strings that did not get a copy pass. A cold visitor reads them as "we have not decided yet."
10. **The Companion section's eyebrow.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:104` - "the magical moment". Naming the moment "the magical moment" out loud is the page telling me it is magical. I have not felt anything yet. Show me the moment, do not announce it.

## What rings false

1. **"Tibbly is the only OSRS plugin you'd say goodbye to."** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:234`. Read it out loud. A player who has played for ten years would not say this about a plugin they installed last week. The line is aspirational and the page knows it, but the page is asserting it at me on first read. It should be earned in the FAQ, in a testimonial, or by a creator. Said by the marketing site itself, it sounds like the slogan a deck shows to a VC.
2. **"How'd that Sins of the Father attempt go?"** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:95` and `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:51`. The framing is "Logs in for the first time in three days." Sins of the Father is the kind of quest where you would remember exactly what happened and would not need the AI to ask. A better remembered-quest beat would be a specific blocker the player hit: "You were two ribs short of the Verzik kill last Tuesday. Want me to dig up the run?" The current line is a generic "checking in", which is what Clippy would say. The `docs/marketing/BRAND_VOICE.md:64-66` rule "Lead with the action, not the explanation" is broken here by the marketing site itself.
3. **"You don't need an antifire this time. Your fire cape's plenty."** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:96` and `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:55`. The situation is "Banking before a fresh trip to King Black Dragon." A fire cape does not let you skip antifire at KBD - KBD's dragonfire is reduced but not eliminated by fire cape; the wiki-fluent player knows this. If the companion is the "clever friend who already read the wiki" at `docs/marketing/BRAND_VOICE.md:14`, the friend is wrong here. The line is the kind of plausible-but-wrong claim that gets the product roasted on `/r/2007scape`.
4. **The Vorkath line.** `apps/marketing/public/og-card-companion.svg:50` again, separately from the pronoun issue. "Protect magic. She nukes 50s the second the orb spawns." Vorkath does not "nuke 50s" with the magic orb specifically; the magic ball deals up to about 50 damage if prayer is not flicked, but a player saying "nukes 50s" is using language the player would use for an AOE, not a single-target prayer-counter mechanic. The line passes the engineer's vibe check; it fails the Vorkath veteran's vibe check.
5. **"Want me to watch the wave timer?"** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:94`. At Fight Caves wave 47, no one would phrase this as "watch the wave timer". They would say "track waves", "call wave numbers", or just "tell me when 62 is next". "Wave timer" reads as a chatbot trying to be helpful with a feature name. A real friend would say "want me to call your waves?"
6. **The "what makes it alive" capsule headings.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:71-91`. "Grounded in real state" is the language of an engineering RFC, not of an OSRS friend. "Voice changes over time" is the language of a product roadmap. These are the four bullets that should be the warmest sentences on the page; instead they read like the description on a Replika feature comparison table.
7. **"Each tier has a hard cap sized to how you play."** `apps/marketing/src/sections/FAQ.tsx:41`. "Sized to how you play" is meaningless. Either give me a number or stop the sentence at "hard cap". The current version sounds like marketing safety language.
8. **The personality-archetype description for Sardonic veteran.** `apps/backend/src/companion/archetypes.ts:208` - "You have seen every gimmick this game has run since 2013 and you have opinions about most of them." OSRS players know the game launched in 2013 and the line lands well. But the next archetype, Earnest helper at `apps/backend/src/companion/archetypes.ts:233`, says "You are earnest, attentive, and visibly invested in what the player is trying to do." That is HR language. No OSRS friend describes themselves that way. The archetypes have not been ear-tested against the brand voice at `docs/marketing/BRAND_VOICE.md:14-25`.

## What I want that's missing

1. **A creator quote.** No vouching presence on the page. The product is selling "your OSRS friend" and the page is the only one speaking. One sentence from an OSRS YouTuber I recognize (J1mmy, Settled, Framed, Skiddler, Manked) would do more than every line of body copy on the site.
2. **A specific 30-day relationship arc.** The page tells me the companion remembers me but does not show me what week 4 looks like. "Day 1 it doesn't know you. Day 30 it knows you avoid GotR because you find it boring." I want to see the relationship as a timeline, not as a feature.
3. **What my Iron friend's companion looks like.** The companion is sold as personalized, but the page never shows me two companions next to each other. If my friend picks the heavy-armor Probe with sardonic-veteran and I pick research-array with earnest helper, do they sound different in the same situation? Show me the same prompt answered four ways.
4. **The "I will not pretend to be human" pose.** `docs/product/EMBODIED_COMPANION.md:198` mentions the parasocial risk and the disclosure floor. The marketing page does not mention this. For a cold visitor in 2026 worried about AI relationships, "the companion is warm but will tell you it is not a person" is a feature, not a footnote. Put it on the page.
5. **The privacy lockup.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/FAQ.tsx:46` is a good answer to "what about my privacy" but it lives inside an accordion below the fold. A cold visitor who screenshots the page for their Discord wants the privacy posture visible without clicking. One line near the hero: "Game state never persists past the reply." That is the bumper-sticker.
6. **A real Fight Caves clip.** `docs/product/COMPANION_VISUAL_BIBLE.md:606-622` specs the 4-second hero loop with the herb scan. It is not on the marketing site yet. The companion section is text vignettes, not motion. Until the loop ships, the "feel alive" claim is just claim.
7. **What happens when I cancel.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/FAQ.tsx:49-51` mentions refunds. It does not mention what happens to the companion. Does it forget me? Does it sit there silent until I resubscribe? Does it leave Lumbridge? This is the relationship product. The breakup matters.
8. **A "show, don't tell" naming moment.** The page tells me I can rename the companion but does not show me one named anything other than "Tibbly". Show me three real player nicknames in the wild (Boaty, Pip, Norm) so I imagine my own.
9. **The first-install in-game flow as a screenshot.** The "ask Tibbly" CTA at `docs/product/COMPANION_VISUAL_BIBLE.md:664-684` is a strong moment. It is not on the page. I want to see the bubble "hi. i'm tibbly. pick how i look, then ask me anything." rendered over a real Lumbridge background. That image is the conversion image.

## My first 30 seconds, written as a stream-of-consciousness

I open the tab from a `/r/2007scape` link. The og-card preloaded a brown sphere with a yellow dot and a speech bubble that says Vorkath is a "she". I roll my eyes a little. I land on the page. The H1 is "Stop alt-tabbing. Start playing." Okay, that is a good promise, I have alt-tabbed to the wiki nine times tonight. Below it: "Tibbly reads your bank, your quest log, and your inventory. It never moves your character, never clicks a tile, never types in chat." Good, that is the bot question answered up front. There is a chatbox to the right with a Dragon Slayer 2 example and the answer says "bank tab 7, I tagged it." Okay, that is the magic. I am 12 seconds in and I am tracking. I see a little brown ball with a yellow eye floating in the corner with a speech bubble. The bubble cycles to "Protect magic. She nukes 50s the second the orb spawns." Wait, she? Vorkath is a he. I scroll down. There is a "Companion" section with five quoted lines and the eyebrow "the magical moment". The third line is "How'd that Sins of the Father attempt go?" which is the most generic check-in line I have ever read. The fourth one says I do not need an antifire at KBD because my fire cape is plenty, which is wrong. I am out at second 28. I close the tab. I might come back if a creator I trust posts about it.

## My first 5 minutes in-plugin (assuming the placeholder Probe sprite)

I install the plugin from sideload. RuneLite restarts. I open the config panel and find a "Tibbly Companion" section. I see "Show Tibbly companion" is on by default. I see "Companion form" with four options: Veteran, Fox, Wisp, Golem. I pick Wisp because I like the floaty feel. I tab back to the game. The companion appears next to my character. It is a brown sphere with a yellow circle eye. I had picked "Wisp". The CONFIG.md said this would be "A floating moth-wisp with a face. Soft, glowing, expressive." It is a brown ball. Already the gap between what I was told and what I see is undermining my trust. I move my character toward the Lumbridge graveyard. The companion follows. It bobs a bit. The "first-install" bubble fades in: "hi. i'm tibbly. pick how i look, then ask me anything." Okay, the bubble is fine, the line is calm, I like the lowercase. But I already picked how it looks and it looks like a ball. I type "/tibbly what should I do tonight" in the chat. The bubble opens and the answer comes back. It tells me to do the Wintertodt because my Firemaking is 78 and the boss is good gp/hr for my level. The fact is right. The voice is fine. But the companion next to me is still a brown ball. After three minutes I open the config again. I see I am locked into one of four Probes. I hit the Settings cog to switch to "Veteran" (the default) hoping the hooded humanoid I saw on the marketing page is the real visual. It is the same brown ball. The "humanoid" description in CONFIG.md was about the *old* visual direction; the bible got pivoted to "all four are Probes". The plugin docs and the config labels (Veteran / Fox / Wisp / Golem) are out of sync with the actual sprite atlas. I am five minutes in and the product has misled me twice. I leave the companion on for the rest of the session because I want to see one proactive line, but the cooldown discipline means I do not see one for another seven minutes. I log out without paying.

## What would land "Tibbly is the only OSRS plugin you'd say goodbye to" for me

One specific moment, not a feature list. The moment I would believe that line is the moment the companion says, on session start at week three of my subscription, something like: "you've been chasing fire cape for nine days. last attempt died on wave 53 to mage-jad spawn order. i logged the spawn order. want it before you head in?" That moment is "this is mine, this knows what I am doing, this remembers what the wiki cannot remember about me." The page should show me that exact moment. The companion remembering my fire cape attempt history specifically, in voice, is the only thing that earns the goodbye line. The current five vignettes at `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:92-98` come close on lines 1 and 5 but the other three are generic check-ins. Replace them with three more specific "remembered-history" beats and the line stops feeling slogan-y.

## Top 5 specific changes I'd make

1. **Fix the og-card line and the pronoun split before anything else.** `apps/marketing/public/og-card-companion.svg:50` and `apps/marketing/public/og-card-companion.svg:1` and `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:110`. The og-card is the cold-open. Replace "Protect magic. She nukes 50s the second the orb spawns." with "Switch prayer. Fireball in two ticks." which sets the timing language the brand voice doc anchors against at `docs/marketing/BRAND_VOICE.md:14`. Pick `it` or `she` once and use it everywhere. This single change saves the Reddit share moment. Unlocks: trust on first impression.
2. **Re-author the five magical-moment lines under the eye of a Vorkath / KBD / Fight Caves player.** `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:92-98`. Lines 2, 3, and 4 ring false to a player who has done the content. Lines should be checked against the actual mechanics (KBD still needs anti-fire; Fight Caves "wave timer" is "wave call"; Sins of the Father check-in is generic). Replace each with a *specific remembered detail* the wiki could not know, in the voice the brand-voice doc anchors against. Unlocks: the magical-moment payoff actually pays off.
3. **Reconcile the four-starter promise with the four-Probe reality, in the config and in the marketing.** `apps/plugin/docs/CONFIG.md:106-116` and `apps/plugin/src/main/kotlin/co/rowm/osrsllm/companion/CompanionConfig.kt:24-58` and `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx`. Either rename the starter labels to match the post-pivot Probe variants (Probe-Standard, Probe-Visor, Probe-Armor, Probe-Array), or admit on the marketing page that the visual is a floating robot in four flavours, not four creatures. The current state is the player being told one thing on the site and finding another in the panel. Unlocks: the placeholder sprite stops being a surprise.
4. **Ship the 4-second hero loop or remove the "feel alive" claim.** `docs/product/COMPANION_VISUAL_BIBLE.md:606-622` specs it. Until it lands, the "what makes it feel alive" capsule at `apps/marketing/src/sections/Companion.tsx:200-224` is talk. Even a static frame of the companion mid-scan over a real OSRS background would beat the current text-only treatment. Unlocks: the marketing page stops telling and starts showing.
5. **Flip `proactiveTriggersEnabled` to default-off on first install, with a one-time bubble that earns the upgrade.** `apps/plugin/src/main/kotlin/co/rowm/osrsllm/companion/CompanionConfig.kt:30`. The first session should be silent presence. After the first time the player clicks the sprite or types `/tibbly`, ask "want me to chime in when something useful comes up?" and let them opt in. The cooldown discipline at `apps/plugin/src/main/kotlin/co/rowm/osrsllm/companion/CompanionDialogueOrchestrator.kt:212-222` is excellent but starts from a position the player did not consent to. Make the proactive layer the player's choice, and the first proactive line becomes a relationship beat instead of an interruption. Unlocks: "alive but respects me" instead of "AI that won't shut up", which is the difference between paying and uninstalling.

## Wins

- The H1 "Stop alt-tabbing. Start playing." at `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:138` is the best sales sentence on the page. Do not touch it.
- The bot/ban FAQ answer at `apps/marketing/src/sections/FAQ.tsx:16` cites specific competitor plugin counts. That is the kind of community-fluent answer a real player wrote.
- The Hub-rejection callout at `apps/marketing/src/sections/FAQ.tsx:56` is honest in a way that builds trust. Most product pages would hide a prior rejection. This one names it.
- The cooldown discipline in `apps/plugin/src/main/kotlin/co/rowm/osrsllm/companion/CompanionDialogueOrchestrator.kt:88-122` (min-gap, hourly budget, density penalty, session decay) is the right shape. Every gate has a reason. The defaults at 30s min-gap and 8 lines per hour are the right floor.
- The `dryWikiVeteran` archetype prompt at `apps/backend/src/companion/archetypes.ts:155-168` reads true to the brand voice. "Treat the player as a competent adult. No exclamation marks, no toxic positivity, no AI-disclaim, no Have-a-great-day signoffs." That is the voice the marketing copy should already be in.
- The Iron-tier-hidden-behind-email move at `apps/marketing/src/sections/PricingTiers.tsx:226-234` is confident pricing. Keep it hidden.
- The `BRAND_VOICE.md:124-133` "Quote their own inventory back at them" rule is the moat. Every magical-moment line should be measured against it before it ships.
- The hero's sample exchange at `apps/marketing/src/sections/Hero.tsx:222-251` ("bank tab 7, I tagged it") is the single most convincing claim on the page. Promote it.
- The two-document discipline (EMBODIED_COMPANION.md plus COMPANION_VISUAL_BIBLE.md) at the spec level is serious work. The product team has thought about this carefully. The miss is between spec and surface, not in the spec itself.
