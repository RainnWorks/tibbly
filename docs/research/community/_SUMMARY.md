## The three highest-conviction pain points

1. **"What do I do next on this quest?"** — Quest Helper is the #1
   most-installed RuneLite plugin at **555,505 installs** and OSRS now
   has **180 quests** (24 free + 156 members) with multi-skill / multi-
   prereq chains. An LLM that ALSO knows your bank, equipment, and
   inventory does what Quest Helper can't.
2. **"The wiki is a tab-out tax."** — WikiSync (302K installs) + Loot
   Lookup (140K installs) prove that hundreds of thousands of players
   are paying an attention cost on every wiki round-trip. A grounded
   chat answers in-client.
3. **"Boss mechanics are a memorization wall."** — Eight of the top-30
   plugins are boss-specific helpers (Tombs of Amascut, Zulrah, Gauntlet,
   Tempoross, CoX, Fight Cave Waves, Hunllef Helper, Fight Caves Spawn
   Predictor). Each plugin is hand-written for one fight. An LLM with
   live animation / NPC tracking handles *every* fight, including
   ones nobody has written a plugin for yet.

(Full P1-P13 list with sourced evidence in `pain-points.md`.)

## Headline candidates for marketing

Sorted by punch / pillar.

- **"Your bank knows. Your assistant should too."** — leads on the gear
  / inventory pain (P3).
- **"Stop alt-tabbing. Start playing."** — leads on the wiki tab-out tax
  (P2). Strongest hook for casual players.
- **"Quest Helper for every quest. Including the ones you'd never
  finish."** — leads on P1, anchors directly against the #1 plugin.
- **"Knows every boss. Even the ones released yesterday."** — leads on
  P4, the moment-driven angle.
- **"Your Leagues 6 strategist, live in-client."** — seasonal headline,
  burn after Demonic Pacts ends.

Primary recommendation: **"Stop alt-tabbing. Start playing."** Pairs
cleanly with the 5-second hero demo (player asks a question, AI
highlights the NPC, player walks over).
