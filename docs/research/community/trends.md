# What's hot in OSRS right now

Researcher: agent r2 (RAI-6). Date: 2026-06-21.

The current cycle is dominated by three things: **Leagues 6 (Demonic
Pacts)**, the **Sailing skill** maturing into its first full content
season, and **Varlamore Final Dawn** content (Doom of Mokhaiotl, Gemstone
Crab, Auburn Valley) continuing to absorb mid-game players.

---

## Leagues 6 — Demonic Pacts

- Started in 2026, themed around contracts with modified Echo bosses.
- Starts in **Varlamore** (a first — every prior league started in
  Misthalin), which forces players to relearn early-game pathing.
- Builds on the Echo Bosses introduced in Leagues 5 (Raging Echoes,
  2024-25) — "stronger regional boss variants dropping powerful
  equipment".
- Wiki notes: "The page notes potential issues with 'discontinued items'
  since league trophies are unobtainable after events end."
- Source: https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Leagues

**Marketing implication.** Our biggest seasonal traffic spike. A
Leagues-mode toggle that re-rolls the system prompt for "Demonic Pacts
rules, modified Echo boss mechanics, Varlamore start" turns the product
into a Leagues-strategy assistant for a month-long buy window. People
buy seasonal apps for things they would never pay for permanently —
fitness apps, fantasy-football tools, league-specific OSRS sites — and
this is the same shape.

## Sailing skill — first content season

- Released **2025-11-19**. Required quest: **Pandemonium** (short novice
  quest, 300 base Sailing XP).
- **83,589 players hit 99 Sailing as of June 2026** — first 99 inside
  four days of release.
- **27 islands** added, three boat types (raft / skiff / sloop), boat
  facilities, crewmate hiring, deep-sea trawling, cannon combat.
- Patch history shows continual rebalance: collision bug fixes, cannon
  tweaks, XP-rate adjustments, deep-sea trawling rewards doubled in
  **May 2026**.
- Plugin-hub demand: **Sailing** plugin — 341,295 installs (5th overall)
  and **Port Tasks** — 228,616 installs.
- Sources:
  - https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Sailing
  - https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pandemonium
  - https://runelite.net/plugin-hub

**Marketing implication.** A new skill = a new "I don't know what I'm
doing" market. We should ship a Sailing-specialized system prompt that
includes boat-build trade-offs, port-task routing, and trawling XP
math. Easy demo: "what should I do at 67 Sailing for fastest 80?"

## Varlamore Final Dawn — third expansion

- Released **2025-07-23**. New areas: Tlati Rainforest, Auburn Valley,
  Custodia Pass.
- New bosses: **Doom of Mokhaiotl** (delve-style, devs say comparable to
  Corrupted Gauntlet at base, "really, really tough" at deep delves) and
  **Gemstone Crab** (low-intensity, mid-game co-op).
- New minigame: Vale Totems fletching in Auburn Valley.
- Plugin-hub demand: **House Thieving Varlamore** — 172,737 installs;
  **Totem Fletching** — 197,696 installs.
- Sources:
  - https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Varlamore
  - https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Doom_of_Mokhaiotl

**Marketing implication.** Doom of Mokhaiotl is *the* boss to demo against
because (a) it has delve-progression, which gives the LLM a great
ramp-narrative ("I just want to clear delve 5") and (b) deep delves are
hard enough that an AI prayer-caller is genuinely useful.

## Other ambient trends

- **Quest count crossed 180** (24 free + 156 members) as of 20 May 2026.
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Quest
- **WikiSync hit ~300K installs**, indicating the wiki ecosystem still
  has demand from a third of the active RuneLite player base for *more*
  wiki integration. https://runelite.net/plugin-hub
- **GIM / group play** continues to grow — Hub Party Panel at 231K
  installs is a proxy.
- **AFK-method optimization** stays the dominant content category on
  YouTube — Mahogany Homes, Sailing port tasks, Mastering Mixology, and
  Hunter Rumours all crack the top 25 plugins.

## Calendar opportunities

- **Leagues 6 closing month** — surge in "what should I do with my last
  weekend" queries. Ship a "last-week sprint planner" template.
- **Sailing anniversary (Nov 2026)** — "one year of Sailing" retrospective
  content; partner with creators.
- **New raid / quest drops** — sponsor first-week content. Cool-down
  cycle is short; AI shines when no plugin exists yet.
- **Christmas / Easter events** — short, dense, completionist-driven; AI
  step-through is the ideal use case.
